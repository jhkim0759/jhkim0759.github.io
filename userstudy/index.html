Indoor Scene Study

20 scenesone choice per sceneabout 5–10 minutes

Each screen shows one photo of a room and four 3D scenes, A to D, built from that photo by different methods. Every scene is rendered from the camera position of the photo, so you can compare it with the photo directly.

Your task: pick the best scene

Which 3D scene best matches the photo and is the best-built room?

Judge both together. The scene should contain the objects in the photo, arranged as in the photo, and it should be a convincing room: objects rest on the floor or a support, and nothing floats or cuts through other objects or the walls.

  • Pick exactly one of A–D on every screen. If two look equally good, choose the one you lean toward.
  • Click a scene, or press its key, to choose it. The study then moves to the next scene by itself, and your answers are submitted right after the last scene. The top bar shows how many scenes are left.
  • Keys: 1–4 choose A–D, Back returns to the previous scene, where you can change your answer.

Your answers are kept in this browser after every scene, so you can close the page and continue later.
